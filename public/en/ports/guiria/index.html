About the port

Güiria lies on the Paria Peninsula, at the eastern end of Sucre State, facing the Gulf of Paria. It is a key point for fishing activity and for logistics support to offshore operations in the region. Its proximity to Trinidad and Tobago makes it strategic for regional traffic.

Services we coordinate in Güiria

  • Husbandry: vessel and owner attendance
  • Logistics support for offshore operations
  • Arrival and departure coordination with the port agent
  • Crew changes and transfers
  • Launch services
  • Supplies and deliveries on board
  • Customs clearance coordination

Frequently asked questions

Who is the port agent in Güiria?

A licensed local partner agency in the Güiria jurisdiction handles the official formalities as port agent. MARIMAR provides husbandry and coordinates the whole call as your single point of contact.

What does the husbandry service include?

Looking after the needs of the vessel and owner during the call: crew changes, spare parts and supplies, medical assistance, hotels, transport and reporting to the owner.

Do you support vessels and offshore projects in the Gulf of Paria?

Yes. We coordinate agency, launches, crew and supplies for vessels and projects operating in the Gulf of Paria.

What do I need to request a quote?

Vessel name, ETA, type of operation and services required. With that we send you the proforma disbursement account (PDA).
