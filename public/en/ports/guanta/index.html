About the port

The International Port of Guanta, in Anzoátegui State near Barcelona and Puerto La Cruz, is operated by Bolipuertos. It is the main commercial port in eastern Venezuela, handling containers, general cargo and bulk for the region.

Services we coordinate in Guanta

  • Vessel agency and attendance
  • Import and export customs clearance
  • Container and general cargo coordination
  • Arrival and departure notices to the Harbour Master
  • Crew changes and transfers
  • Supplies and deliveries on board
  • Cargo and vehicle surveys (Survey Division)

Frequently asked questions

Does MARIMAR operate directly in Guanta?

The call is handled through a licensed partner agency in the Guanta jurisdiction. MARIMAR is your single point of contact: we send the PDA, coordinate with our partner and follow up the operation end to end.

Do you handle container customs clearance in Guanta?

Yes. Customs clearance is handled with our licensed partner in the jurisdiction, while MARIMAR coordinates the documentation and follows up the process.

Can you survey cargo on arrival at Guanta?

Yes. Our Survey Division independently records the condition of cargo and vehicles and issues a report for insurance and claims.

What do I need to quote a call at Guanta?

Vessel name, ETA, cargo type and volume, and the services required. With that we send you the PDA.
