About the port

Puerto La Cruz, in Anzoátegui State, hosts a refinery and an oil terminal, and sits next to the Port of Guanta. It is a frequent call for tankers, support vessels and coastal craft.

Services we coordinate in Puerto La Cruz

  • Husbandry for tankers and support vessels
  • Arrival and departure coordination with the port agent
  • Crew changes and transfers
  • Launch services
  • Supplies, spare parts and deliveries on board
  • Hotels and transport
  • Condition surveys (Survey Division)

Frequently asked questions

Who is the port agent in Puerto La Cruz?

A licensed local partner agency in the Puerto La Cruz jurisdiction handles the official formalities as port agent. MARIMAR provides husbandry and coordinates the whole call as your single point of contact.

What does the husbandry service include?

Looking after the needs of the vessel and owner during the call: crew changes, spare parts and supplies, medical assistance, hotels, transport and reporting to the owner.

Do you attend tankers in Puerto La Cruz?

Yes. We coordinate agency, crew, launches and supplies for tankers and support vessels.

Can you deliver spare parts on board?

Yes. We coordinate receipt, customs clearance and delivery on board of spare parts and supplies.

How do I request a proforma disbursement account?

Send us the vessel, ETA and services by WhatsApp or email and we reply with the PDA.
