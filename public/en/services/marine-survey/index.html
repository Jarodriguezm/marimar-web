Marine and cargo surveys at every port in Venezuela

What we survey

Vehicles

VIN-by-VIN records at loading and discharge: damage, missing items, accessories and a standard photo protocol.

General and project cargo

Condition, quantity and damage. Supervision of loading, discharge, stowage and lashing.

Containers

Container condition, seals, stuffing and stripping.

Draft surveys

Determination of cargo loaded or discharged by draft readings.

On-hire / off-hire

Vessel condition and bunkers on board at delivery and redelivery.

Vessel condition and damage

Vessel and craft inspections, and damage reports for insurers and claims.

How we work

Request

Tell us the type of survey, port, cargo and estimated date.

Independent report

You receive a technical report with evidence for your operation, insurer or claim.

Why an independent survey

Evidence that holds

A dated, photographed record made by a third party carries more weight with carriers, ports and insurers.

Origin and destination

Comparing condition at loading and discharge allows damage to be attributed to the leg where it occurred.

Speed

Claims have short time limits, so we inspect before cargo leaves the port area.

Frequently asked questions

Do you survey at any port in Venezuela?

Yes. The Survey Division covers ports and terminals nationwide. We arrange the surveyor's travel according to the operation date.

Why survey at origin as well?

A destination-only survey proves how the cargo arrived, not where it was damaged. With an origin record, damage can be attributed to the transport leg.

Is the report valid for insurance claims?

Yes. It is an independent technical report with photos and damage descriptions, designed for insurance and claims.

How far in advance should I request it?

As early as possible, ideally once you know the loading or arrival date, to secure the surveyor's attendance.
