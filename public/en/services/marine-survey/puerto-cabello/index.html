About the port

Puerto Cabello, in Carabobo State, is Venezuela's main commercial port by cargo volume. It handles containers, general cargo, bulk and vehicles, and is close to the El Palito refinery.

Common surveys at Puerto Cabello

  • Container and general cargo inspections
  • Vehicle surveys at loading and discharge
  • Draft surveys for bulk cargo
  • Damage reports for insurance and claims

Frequently asked questions

Do you carry out draft surveys at Puerto Cabello?

Yes. We determine cargo loaded or discharged by draft readings, with an independent report.

Why survey at origin as well?

A destination-only survey proves how the cargo arrived, not where it was damaged. With an origin record, damage can be attributed to the transport leg.

Is the report valid for insurance claims?

Yes. It is an independent technical report with photos and damage descriptions, designed for insurance and claims.
